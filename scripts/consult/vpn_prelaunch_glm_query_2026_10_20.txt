═══════════════════════════════════════════════ money model (60-second primer)

- LedgerEntry is append-only; ONE OWNER PER ENTRY resolved as: account_id ->
  that account; else group_id -> group-only; else customer_id. Roll-ups are
  sums of the level below (services.MoneyBook). Charges positive, credits
  negative. posted + pending = net_owed.
- Billing modes: payg bills used_traffic - usage_baseline (metered).
  prepay bills data_limit - billed_data_limit (the package, at sale).
  A grouped account's EFFECTIVE mode is always its GROUP's
  (services.effective_billing_mode); its own field only matters standalone.
- usage_baseline is rolled ONLY by payg-side settles/resets, activation, the
  payg cap-hit handler, external-reset detection, and delete close-out.
  NO prepay settle path ever touches it. billed_data_limit is written by
  prepay settles (`billed_data_limit = data_limit or 0`) and by
  neutralize_billed_baseline (D21 guard, payg->prepay direction only).
- QueuedPlan lifecycle: auto-queued by sync_job for effective-prepay,
  auto_renew_enabled accounts near quota/expiry (no billing_mode stored);
  or set by hand via POST /api/accounts/{id}/next-plan (operator, any
  account). Activated by sync_job when Marzban reports status
  limited/expired AND a pending plan exists — sync_job checks NEITHER
  billing mode NOR group membership at activation time. Activation
  auto-settles the old plan (billable_bytes at the CURRENT effective rate),
  applies new data_limit + expire=now+duration, resets usage,
  sets billed_data_limit=0, and optionally switches the account's own
  billing_mode if the plan carries one.
- Concurrency: single-process backend, SQLite. @serialise_billing
  (asyncio billing_lock) wraps every settle/reset/money endpoint; sync_job
  takes _sync_lock then billing_lock. PTB bots run WITHOUT
  concurrent_updates (updates sequential). Shop purchase paths use
  per-user asyncio locks + in-lock balance/status re-reads; topup approval
  uses a conditional UPDATE (rowcount) against status=pending.
- Two economies: the shop wallet and the reseller ledger are separate.
  Shop linking is REFUSED for payg customers (routers/shop.py
  _customer_has_payg_billing) but ALLOWED for prepay ones; a linked
  customer's own accounts are extendable in place by shop purchases
  (shop_service.renewable_account's linked-customer fallback).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════ PART A — CLAIMS TO VERIFY

CLAIM 1 (HIGH) — prepay->payg flips leave a stale usage_baseline; the next
payg settle re-bills consumption already covered by the prepaid package.
  - No guard on the prepay->payg direction in: groups.py update_group
    (~line 207, comment says "prepay->payg changes nothing (payg never
    reads billed_data_limit...)"), accounts.py update_relationship
    (~line 577), accounts.py update_billing (~line 631).
  - services.py billable_bytes payg branch reads
    max(0, used_traffic - usage_baseline); usage_baseline is 0 (or
    creation/last-payg-era value) for a prepay-lived account because no
    prepay settle rolls it.
  - Scenario: prepay group, 60GB package at 1000T/GB, settled
    (billed=60GB, baseline stays 0), customer burns 40GB, operator flips
    group to payg -> pending jumps to 40,000T; next settle posts it.
    Amplified: payg_monthly_job.py auto-settles every payg group at each
    Jalali month end (line ~121), so the phantom charge posts unattended.
  - Also verify: test_group_mode_flip_stress.py pins the wrong behavior
    (asserts the post-flip figure is "real metered pending") but its
    scenario never posts ledger rows, so it cannot distinguish covered
    usage from genuinely-unbilled usage.

CLAIM 2 (MEDIUM) — pending QueuedPlans survive prepay->payg flips on the
two paths today's fix missed:
  - accounts.py update_relationship (move from a prepay group into a payg
    group) does not call cancel_pending_queued_plan.
  - groups.py update_group flipping a whole prepay group to payg leaves
    EVERY member's auto-queued plan pending (auto_renew_enabled defaults
    True, so prepay members commonly carry one).
  - On the member's next Marzban expire (prepay rows keep their expire),
    sync_job activates the stale plan: charges the old plan at the now-payg
    effective rate (stacking with CLAIM 1's charge), re-imposes a package
    shape + fresh 31-day expire on a metered-billed member, resets usage.
    When that expire passes with no plan behind it the member is
    disconnected and _maybe_settle_payg_cap_hit cannot rescue them
    (remaining_gb > 0; its retry path only toggles status against a
    past-dated expire).
  - Explicitly NOT a gap (verify you agree): settle_group /
    settle_group_member / settle_account need no cancel_pending_queued_plan
    — a queued plan is supposed to survive settles (activation keys on
    Marzban limited/expired status, which a settle does not produce).

CLAIM 3 (MEDIUM, conditional) — shop extension of a LINKED prepay
customer's account grows data_limit without touching billed_data_limit:
  - shop_service.py has ZERO occurrences of billed_data_limit (grep).
    _extend_order (~line 1548) computes
    target_limit = int(limit) + bytes_from_gb(order.data_limit_gb);
    _record_extended mirrors via sync_marzban_fields only.
  - models.py ~line 61 claims "the reseller ledger never sees them"
    (two-economies rule); the payg link gate (routers/shop.py
    _customer_has_payg_billing) refuses payg customers but ALLOWS prepay
    ones; renewable_account's linked-customer fallback has no mode filter.
  - Scenario: linked customer, 50GB prepay account, billed_data_limit=50GB,
    pending 0. Buys +20GB in shop (wallet debited) -> data_limit=70GB,
    billed_data_limit still 50GB -> reseller pending = 20GB x rate -> next
    prepay settle charges it AGAIN (wallet + ledger double payment).
  - Contrast: delegate_service.renew line ~295 DOES increment
    billed_data_limit by the added GB — the codebase knows the pattern;
    the shop path lacks it. This was inside today's "clean" pass for
    extend — your task is to confirm or refute the interaction specifically.

CLAIM 4 (MEDIUM, conditional) — delegate renew has no billing-mode gate;
