    Rebuild the money model around one owner per ledger entry, so every balance in the UI reconciles by construction
    
    Reported: recording a member's payment against their own account turned the
    whole group into a 270,000 creditor, while every member row underneath it
    still showed money owed. Also, an account that had already paid still printed
    "243,916 not invoiced yet" under "13,916 owed" -- a breakdown that contradicts
    itself, because in isolation it omits the payment that cancels most of it.
    
    Both came from the same structural flaw, not from display logic. A ledger
    entry carried customer_id, group_id AND account_id at once, and each screen
    summed whichever column it cared about. The group summed group_id; the member
    row summed account_id; the customer summed customer_id. So a member's payment
    counted in the group's pool AND in that member's own balance, while the
    matching charge sat in a different bucket -- the two never cancelled, and no
    two levels ever had to agree.
    
    Replaced the ad-hoc per-scope sums with an explicit model, documented at the
    top of services.py and enforced by services.MoneyBook:
    
      ONE OWNER PER ENTRY, resolved in order:
        account_id set -> that account's money
        else group_id  -> group money not tied to a member
        else customer  -> customer money not tied to an account
      The other columns still record who was invoiced and still drive the history
      views, but are never summed into a balance.
    
      ROLL-UPS ARE SUMS OF THE LEVEL BELOW, never an independent re-query:
        account  = its own entries (+ its uninvoiced usage, for net)
        group    = sum of members + group-only entries
        customer = sum of owned accounts + sum of represented groups + own entries
    
    A group's figure can now no longer disagree with the member rows printed under
    it, because it is literally computed as their sum. Every page (accounts,
    groups, group detail, customers, customer detail, dashboard, finance, ledger
    balance) reads from one MoneyBook snapshot per request, so two numbers on one
    screen cannot come from reads taken a query apart.
    
    UI follows the same rule: one netted "Owes now" everywhere, never a pair of
    competing figures. The group page's "Pending" + "Settled balance" cards --
    which together claimed a credit while the members owed -- are now "Owes now"
    (the members' sum) plus "Not invoiced yet". The Accounts table drops the
    contradictory secondary line in favour of a tooltip carrying the full
    arithmetic, which always adds up. Recording a payment on a customer who owns
    exactly one account now attributes it to that account, so the money lands
    where the operator is looking instead of at a level above it.
    
    Wrote a ten-invariant reconciliation suite (roll-up identities at each level,
    group-never-flips-to-credit, no leak from a group onto its representative's
    own account, every entry counted exactly once globally, API responses match
    the book, settling never changes what anyone owes, plus 300 randomised
    entries across all three scopes). It caught a further real bug: "payment
    received now too" silently did nothing when the cycle had no new usage,
    because the credit was nested inside the charge branch -- while the dialog's
    preview promised a settled balance. Marking paid now clears whatever is
    genuinely outstanding, including group-level debt with no member to attribute
    it to.
    
    All eleven verification suites pass, backend imports clean, frontend tsc -b
    and production build clean.
